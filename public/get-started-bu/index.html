Things to know

Before you get here

Comfortable clothes you can move in.
A second pair of clean indoor shoes — not the ones you arrived in. (Forgot them? You can train barefoot, but shoes are comfier when you're starting out.)
A water bottle so you can stay hydrated.

I'm at the gym. What now?

1Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in and get set.
2Stash your things in a locker (bring your own lock).
3Read the Rules poster at the entrance.
4Head to the spring floor and join the group warm-up.
Please arrive 15 minutes early to sign in. To be fair to those who arrive on time, there's a 15-minute cutoff — anyone more than 15 minutes late can't be admitted to a class.
Took an Introduction Class? Here's a bonus.
You get 15% off any class or open-gym pass you buy the same day. Just ask the coach at the front desk.
One more thing

A few more questions

The other things people usually ask before their first visit.

Do I need any experience to start?

None at all. Most people who walk in have never done parkour before. Book an Introduction Class or drop into open gym and a coach will start you with the basics at your own pace.

What ages do you welcome?

Everyone from age 3 and up. We run youth classes, adult classes, camps for kids 5+, and open gym for all ages. There is a class and a session for every body.

Do I have to book, or can I just drop in?

Both work. Open Gym and Group Classes need no reservation — just check the schedule and show up. Introduction and Private Classes are reserved ahead of time so a coach is set aside for you.

Is it safe? What about the kids?

Safety is built into the space — padded floors, a foam pit, and trained coaches who scale every skill to your level. Read the Rules poster at the entrance and your coach will guide the rest.

Can I host a birthday or group event?

Absolutely. We run full and mini birthday parties plus group events for schools, teams and organizations. Weekend slots fill fast, so book early — see the Events page for details.
