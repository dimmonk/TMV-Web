01 The program

More than parkour — preparation for any sport.

The Athletic Program is built around two 90-minute coached sessions each week — the core of the program — for ages 9–14 and 15+. It's designed to boost real athletic performance: you'll build the strength, speed and control that carry into any sport you choose.

Two complementary classes round it out — Explosive for power and Speed League for speed and agility — so you can train more of what your sport demands.

Membership includes unlimited open gym to practice at your own pace, plus monthly evaluations with personalized feedback — so progress is tracked, not guessed.

Key benefits
Jumping power

Jump higher and further — basketball, volleyball, track & field.

Agility & speed

Quick reflexes and nimble footwork for soccer, tennis, martial arts.

Body resilience

A body that absorbs impact, lowering injury risk in any activity.

Strength & endurance

The stamina demanding sports like football, rugby and gymnastics need.

Coordination

Sharper body control for dance, skateboarding and climbing.

Faster learning

Skills that make picking up any new sport quicker.

02 Class structure

How a core session runs

Every 90-minute Athletic session follows the same five-part arc — warm up, build, refine, push, recover — so training stays structured and safe.

1
Intro / Warm Up

Dynamic movement and a quick overview of the day to prime body and mind.

2
Physical Conditioning

Strength and endurance work — overall fitness plus parkour-specific power.

3
Technical Foundations

Core parkour technique that builds the base for advanced movement.

4
Theme of the Day

A focused block that rotates each session:

  • Challenges
  • Speed runs
  • Acrobatics
  • Fluidity
5
Stretching / Outro

Stretching and recovery, plus a preview of what the next class brings.
