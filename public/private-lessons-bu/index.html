Good to know
One coach, your whole journey

Sticking with a specific coach means continuity — they know your history, track your progress and build each session on the last.

They come in just for you

Book a specific coach outside their regular shift — even on their day off — and they'll come in only for your session. Coaching on demand, on your schedule.

Each coach sets their rate

Because these are private sessions on their own time, each coach sets their own rate — separate from standard class pricing. The rate shows when you book.

Waiver first

Everyone needs a signed waiver before a first session. New here? Start on the Get Started page, then come back and book.
