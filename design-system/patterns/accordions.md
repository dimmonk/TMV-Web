# Accordions

Two different accordions exist in the export.

1. **Faq component** (`Faq.dc.html`): separate white cards, radius 14, yellow plus that turns
   45 degrees, animated height. Used on Get Started and in the mobile screens.
2. **Events FAQ** (`Events.dc.html`): a two-column layout with a sticky left column and native
   `details` groups, pink group titles, a chevron icon.

Live has both too: `Faq.astro` (Get Started, Camps) and the Events page's own `ev-faq` accordion
(chevron, two columns). Drift item A1 asks which one is the standard.
