# MobileAppBar

The phone chrome's header, below `bp-md`. Sticky `ink` bar.

- Brand screens: logo plus THE MONKEY VAULT, Archivo 900 (live: logo 32px, 14.5px).
- Pages beneath a tab show a yellow back chevron and the page title instead; Back is a real link.
- Right: the open-status pill, Open now in `open-now` on open-now at 13% with a pulsing dot, or Opens at a time in muted white. The time comes from the Members hours.
