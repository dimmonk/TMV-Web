Our camp program is one of our most sought after programs. It is great for all skill levels; from beginner to advanced. Kids will always learn a lot about parkour during the camp but there is always a big focus on having fun in the process. We have a ton of games we play throughout the week. Limited spots available.


Cost: $350.00 per week (HST included)


Location: The Monkey Vault Movement Training Centre, 75 Carl Hall Rd Unit 15.


Ages: Between 6-16 years.


Camp Hours: 9:00am-2:00pm.


Late Pickups: Late pickup is available until 4pm. Cost of late pickup is $20 for the day or $75 for 4-5 days.


Schedule: Schedule will vary day to day but the basic outline is:

  1. Warm up. 2) Structured Lesson. 3) Break. 4) Flips. 5) Lunch. 6) Individual Skills. 7) Break. 8) Games. 9) Cool Down.


Registration: Please make sure to write the name of the participant(s) and add any special instructions if needed. We also require a completed waiver for any new participants.


What To Bring: Participants should bring clean indoor running shoes, a lunch, snacks, a water bottle (labeled), and clothes they feel comfortable moving around in.


Allergies: We ask parents to refrain from packing lunches or snacks that contain any nuts. There may or may not be participants with a nut allergy during the camp. We appreciate your cooperation. Please make sure we are aware of any allergies your kids may have.


Cancellation Policy

We require two weeks' written notice for any cancellations. Please note that fees apply only to refund requests — there are no penalties for rescheduling or for keeping your balance as a credit toward future services.

  • For cancellations with less than two weeks' notice, the 15% deposit will be retained as an administration fee.
  • For cancellations with more than two weeks' notice, a $20 fee will be retained to cover transaction costs, and the remainder will be refunded.
